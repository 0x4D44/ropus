# ROP-BUG-FLUX-00036 — fb2k seek rollback

## Result

Seek and rewind now call `PacketReader::seek_bytes` without taking the wrapper
out of the reader. A failed callback therefore cannot leave `packet_reader`
empty and turn the next operation into an internal panic.

Lazy page-index scans still need temporary ownership of the underlying reader.
Their rollback now always re-seats a `PacketReader`; if cancellation blocks the
rollback, the reader records the logical sample position and retries from the
audio start after cancellation clears. Replaying and discarding to that saved
position avoids losing packets buffered in the original Ogg page.

## Evidence

- Added one-shot seek-callback failures for target seeks and rewinds, with
  successful retry and decode checks.
- Added an abort-during-index test that clears cancellation and compares the
  resumed packet samples with a clean reference decode.
- `cargo clippy -p ropus-fb2k --all-targets --locked -- -D warnings` passed.
- `cargo test -p ropus-fb2k --locked` passed (33 unit tests, 41 integration tests).

The compiler still reports the repository's existing warning that reference DNN
weights are absent in this worktree; it does not affect this crate's tests.

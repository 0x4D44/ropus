## `ropus-fb2k/` pass

The ledger selected `ropus-fb2k/` from the eight remaining never-reviewed areas because it is the external foobar2000/FFI boundary and changed most recently. The pass reviewed `origin/main` at `dcfd694bc90f77cb753eef8daaed400bd24a6462`.

Seven static lenses and a devil's advocate reduced the candidate set to seven defects. The decoder ignores both EOS end trimming and `OpusHead` output gain. Seek and index failures can lose or corrupt the live reader. The duration scan trusts header-shaped payload bytes. Chained decoding runs past the selected stream. Invalid tag keys cross the callback boundary. Success clears the thread-local error with steady-state heap traffic. They are Open as `ROP-BUG-FLUX-00034` through `ROP-BUG-FLUX-00040`.

The repeated Ogg header parsers were explicit, durable structural debt and already carry a source warning against manual synchronization. They became Draft requirement `ROP-REQ-FLUX-00041`.

The pass held partial-index and multiplexing claims as unverified. It rejected full first-seek indexing as signed design and callback atomicity as an unstated contract. Cover-art retention remains a report-only policy/document conflict. Public prose and the periodic seek oracle also need cleanup, but neither justified another ledger item.

No code, build, test, application, decoder, seek, allocator probe, comparison command, or exploratory harness ran. The next bounded pass should review `ropus-tools-core/`, the shared CLI/container layer and owner of the related Ogg parser.

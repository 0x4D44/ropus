# ROP-BUG-FLUX-00056 — reject empty Ogg Opus packets

## Decision

An empty Ogg packet in the Opus audio section is malformed input. It must not
reach the codec: ropus intentionally interprets an empty slice as deliberate
packet-loss concealment. The shared `validate_opus_audio_packet` boundary now
rejects zero-length payloads in direct decode, shared playback/transcode decode,
human and slow-path info scans, and extended TOC collection.

## Evidence

- `cargo test -p ropus-tools-core --lib`: 129 passed.
- `cargo test -p ropus-tools-core --test round_trip`: 22 passed, including a
  generated valid stream with an appended zero-length EOS audio packet. Direct
  decode, playback decode, and extended info all reject it without publishing
  output or fabricating TOC data.
- The build warns that `reference/dnn` is absent in this checkout; this affects
  neural PLC assets only and is unrelated to the malformed-packet guard.

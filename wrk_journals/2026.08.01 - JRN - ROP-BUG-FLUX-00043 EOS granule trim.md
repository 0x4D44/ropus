# ROP-BUG-FLUX-00043 — EOS granule trim

## What changed

- `commands::decode` now selects the serial from `OpusHead`, ignores packets from
  other logical streams, stops at that stream's `last_in_stream()` packet, and
  rejects missing or unknown EOS granules.
- The direct Ogg path validates that decoded samples reach the absolute EOS
  granule, then slices `[pre_skip, eos_granule)` before resampling or writing.
- The shared Symphonia path enables gapless Ogg probing, retains the OpusHead
  pre-skip separately from Symphonia's inferred page delay, requires its
  discovered EOS frame count, and applies the same exact endpoint after decode.
- Integration tests cover a 1,001-sample partial final packet for i16, f32,
  playback, and Opus-to-Opus transcode. A second fixture clears only the final
  page's EOS flag and repairs its CRC; both decode paths reject that complete
  non-EOS stream.

## Validation

- `cargo fmt --all`
- `cargo test -p ropus-tools-core --test round_trip decode_play_and_transcode_trim_to_eos_granule -- --nocapture`
- `cargo test -p ropus-tools-core --test round_trip decode_and_play_reject_physical_eof_before_eos -- --nocapture`
- Existing `round_trip` suite passed before the final full gate; the build emits
  the repository's existing warning that reference DNN assets are absent.

## Notes

Symphonia queues Ogg packets while probing the final page, so its packet-level
`trim_end` can remain zero even when `enable_gapless` is true. The reliable
endpoint is the probed absolute `n_frames` value; slicing after decode keeps
playback, transcode, and direct decode on one RFC 7845 timeline.

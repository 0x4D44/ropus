# ROP-BUG-FLUX-00044 — PLC duration

## What changed

- `commands::decode` now parses each deliberately dropped packet's Opus TOC
  and sizes the PLC buffer from its advertised frame duration and frame count.
- The old 20 ms previous-packet fallback is gone, so first-packet loss and
  transitions between packet durations keep the decoded timeline aligned.
- TOCs with no byte, an incomplete code-3 count, zero frames, overflow, or a
  duration above the decoder's 120 ms limit fail before PLC allocation.
- Both f32 and i16 decode branches check that PLC returned the requested
  per-channel duration.
- Regression coverage includes 10 ms and 60 ms encoded streams, comparing
  no-loss and 100%-loss output lengths, plus unit oracles for code-3 and
  duration-switch TOCs.
